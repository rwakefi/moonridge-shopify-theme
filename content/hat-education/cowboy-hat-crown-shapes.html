The crown is the personality on top of the hat. Same brim, different crease — and suddenly you’re looking at a different person in the mirror.

At Moon Ridge we talk about crown shapes the same way they’re listed on our product pages, so what you read here matches what you shop. Most quality felts and many straws can be reshaped; the names below are the creases you’ll see most often on Stetson, Resistol, and the rest of the wall.

Cattleman’s

The classic. Three dents running front to back with a center crease — the silhouette most people picture when they say “cowboy hat.”

Versatile on nearly every face. A taller cattleman adds presence; a lower one softens the look. If you’re buying your first western hat and don’t know where to start, start here.

Brick / Rounded Brick / Minnick

A squarer, more structured crown with a brick-like top. The rounded brick and Minnick soften those corners without losing the strong front.

Reads modern-western and holds a clean line in photos. Good when you want something more architectural than a soft cattleman.

Pinch Front / Teardrop / Diamond

The front of the crown is pinched into a teardrop or diamond. That front pinch draws the eye up and can lengthen a rounder face.

Common on fashion-forward western and some city looks. If the cattleman feels too “traditional,” try a pinch front before you walk away from western altogether.

Gus / Tom Mix

A dramatic, sloping crease with cowboy-movie DNA — named with Tom Mix for a reason. Higher drama in the front, a strong western story from every angle.

Excellent when you want the hat to make the outfit. Softer on square faces than a hard brick; more statement than a low cattleman.

Texas Punch

A punched, western crease with Texas show-floor energy. Strong front, purposeful dents, built to look finished the moment you put it on.

If you like a hat that already has an opinion, this family’s for you.

Cutter

A working crease with a practical, athletic feel — the kind of crown that looks right in the arena and still cleans up for town.

Less costume, more tool. Pair it with a brim you can live in all day.

Gambler / Telescope

A rounder crown with a telescope-style depression on top. Old-West gambler energy — lower drama in the dents, more in the overall round silhouette.

Works especially well when you want western without a sharp cattleman ridge.

CHL (Cool Hand Luke)

A distinctive crease with a Cool Hand Luke attitude — recognizable once you’ve seen it, hard to mistake for a standard cattleman.

Ask for it by name on the floor; we’ll put it next to a cattleman so you can feel the difference in five seconds.

The Walker

A Moon Ridge–familiar crease with a clean, wearable western line. Think everyday confidence — not a costume crease, not a blank open crown.

When you want “put it on and go,” Walker territory is a strong place to shop.

Mule Kick / Horseshoe

A kicked or horseshoe-style dent pattern with more visual movement across the crown. Fashion-leaning western that still reads authentic when the felt and brim are right.

Charlie 1 Horse and similar lines live happily here.

Open Crown

No crease yet — smooth, round top. This is how every shaped hat begins on the block.

Open crown is the blank canvas. In our Fayetteville hat bar we can steam and set the crease you actually want, matched to your head and face, instead of forcing you into whatever left the factory.

Important: complimentary custom shaping is an in-store service on hats purchased at Moon Ridge. It does not apply to shipped online orders. Bring the open crown in, sit down with us, and we’ll build the crease with you.

How to choose

Match the face. Taller, cleaner creases help round faces. Softer, sloping creases (Gus) ease a strong jaw. Oval faces can wear almost anything — pick the story you want to tell. Full breakdown: Hat Styles for Your Face Shape.

Match the life. Daily wear favors a cattleman or Walker. Stage, show, or photos might want Gus, Texas Punch, or CHL.

Match the head. Crown height and oval vs. round head shape decide whether the hat seals. See Learn Your Head Shape and hat sizing.
