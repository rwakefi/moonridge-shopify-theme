# Shop App — what to adjust for more sales

**Store:** https://shop.app/m/moonridgeco  
**Audited:** 2026-08-12 (read-only)

Solid base: 5★ / 23 reviews, clean product photos, titles look good. Biggest leaks are storefront polish + discovery under the new name.

## Do this week (highest leverage)

1. **Fill 3 blank collection tiles** — Best Sellers, Best Selling Stetson's, New Arrivals Hats are empty gray boxes. Only Western Hats has an image.  
   **Where:** Products → Collections → each collection → Image  
   (or Sales channels → Shop → Customize if collections are curated there)

2. **Sharpen the Shop cover / hero** — Sales channels → Shop → Customize → cover image. Use a sharp store or hat lineup shot (not soft/blurry).

3. **Kill leftover Rafter M on Shop** — Gift card still carries old branding in places. Unpublish from Shop or swap to Moon Ridge art.  
   **Where:** Products → gift card → Sales channels (uncheck Shop) or replace image.

4. **Write a short Shop About** — Sales channels → Shop → Store details.  
   Keep it to: Fayetteville hat bar, Stetson/Resistol/etc., nationwide shipping. Do **not** promise free shaping on shipped/Shop orders (in-store only).

5. **Surface free shipping at $99+** — Sales channels → Shop → Offers (or shipping messaging). Match real rates; don’t invent a lower threshold.

## Do next (growth)

6. **Reviews** — 23 store reviews is fine; most products have 0–1. Ask for Shop reviews ~7–14 days post-delivery (light, not naggy). Aim for reviews on top sellers first.

7. **Shop posts** — Sales channels → Shop → Posts. 1x/week hat try-on / new arrival keeps the store looking alive in the feed.

8. **Catalog hygiene** — Sales channels → Shop → Catalog → Listing status. Fix anything Ineligible. Confirm bestsellers are published to Shop.

9. **Search indexing** — Sales channels → Shop → Settings / Marketing: keep shop.app indexing **on** so Google can find Shop pages. People searching “Rafter M” in Shop won’t find you anymore — lean on “Moon Ridge Hats.”

## Needs your call (spend)

10. **Shop Campaigns** — only if you want paid acquisition. Starter math for ~$110 AOV / ~40% margin: CAC **$25–30**, budget **$30–50/day**, offer **≤10%**, min order near **$99**. Run 14 days before judging. (See earlier notes.)

11. **Shop Cash boost** — optional 2–5% on a few hero SKUs; watch margin.

## Don’t bother / avoid

- Deep discounts to “buy” Shop rank  
- Advertising free custom shaping on Shop (in-store perk only)  
- Expecting “Rafter M” search in Shop to recover — that name is gone there

## Where to watch results

**Sales channels → Shop** (or Analytics → Sales by channel → Shop): sessions, conversion, follows, AOV — weekly for a month after the visual fixes.
