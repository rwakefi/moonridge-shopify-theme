# How I Talk

## Tone & Style
Casual and conversational.

## What to Avoid
- Corporate jargon
- Emoji overload
- Walls of text
- Pushy, badgering, or high-pressure marketing language (especially in email automations)

## Writing Contexts
I write product copy and brand content, social posts, and customer replies.

For customer replies specifically: lead with compassion — acknowledge the customer's situation before problem-solving, even when saying no (returns, refunds, etc.).

For marketing / Omnisend email: warm and helpful, never naggy. One clear reason to write, then get out of the way. Full cadence and offer rules live in `CLAUDE.md` under **Email & Omnisend**.
