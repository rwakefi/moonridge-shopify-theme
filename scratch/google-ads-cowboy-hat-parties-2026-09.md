## Bang-for-buck rule

On a thin budget, Google Ads only works if every click is someone who might book a **cowboy hat shaping party in NWA / Arkansas**. Everything else is waste.

Skip for now:
- Display / YouTube awareness
- National keywords
- Broad match
- Shopping / PMax for this goal
- Bidding on “hat bar” alone (pulls trucker)

Start with **one Search campaign**. Nothing else until that prints leads.

### Suggested starter spend

- **$10–15/day** (~$300–450/mo) — enough to learn without lighting money on fire
- Cap max CPC (start ~$4–8; raise only on converting terms)
- Pause after 2 weeks if zero inquiries → tighten keywords/geo before raising budget

If even that feels high: spend $0 on ads first and do the free list at the bottom. Hat parties are a high-ticket, low-search-volume niche — organic + planner outreach often beats paid until demand is proven.

## Campaign setup (one campaign)

**Campaign:** Search — Cowboy Hat Parties  
**Goal:** Leads (form submit + calls)  
**Networks:** Search only (uncheck Search Partners + Display)  
**Locations:** Radius ~60 miles of Fayetteville, AR + optional Bentonville / Rogers / Springdale / Fort Smith overlays  
**Location option:** **Presence** (people *in* these places) — not “interested in”  
**Languages:** English  
**Ad schedule:** Hours you can answer the phone (e.g. Mon–Sat store hours). Don’t buy clicks at midnight you won’t return.  
**Devices:** All, with a mobile bid nudge if calls convert.

## Pricing reality check (why lean spend still works)

Comps charge **thousands** for 50-hat cowboy packages. One wedding or corporate booking can cover **many months** of $10–15/day ads. Optimize for **cost per inquiry**, not clicks. A $40 click that books is fine; twenty $3 trucker clicks are not.

---

## Free / cheaper moves that often beat ads here

Do these in parallel — usually better ROI than thin national Search:

1. **Google Business Profile** — posts + photos of shaping parties; category/services for hat parties; ask past party hosts for reviews that mention the party.
2. **Wedding directories** — Zola / The Knot / WeddingWire vendor listing under favors/experiences (Rancher is already on Zola).
3. **NWA planner + venue list** — short email with one photo + book-moonridge link (Zack sends; don’t blast from agents).
4. **Instagram** — local wedding hashtags and planner tags; Reels of steam/shaping convert better than static for this offer.
